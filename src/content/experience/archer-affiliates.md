## What Was Going On

So Archer Affiliates came to me with this Amazon affiliates platform that was honestly kind of a mess. The whole thing was outdated, confusing as hell, and basically driving people away instead of getting them to buy stuff. Sales were tanking and they needed someone to fix it.

## What I Did

I jumped in as Lead Frontend Engineer and basically rebuilt the whole thing from scratch. Had to tackle three main things:
- Made the UI/UX actually usable instead of confusing
- Moved everything to Next.js and Material UI so it would actually work properly
- Added some cool new features to get people more engaged

## The Cool Stuff I Built

### Complete Platform Makeover
Did a total redesign that ended up boosting sales by 50%. Pretty wild results, right? Focused on:
- Making it super easy to find and discover affiliate stuff
- Keeping everything looking consistent instead of all over the place
- Mobile-first design because everyone's on their phones anyway
- Better product pages that actually convert

### Support System That Actually Works
Built this ticketing system that completely changed how they handle customer stuff:
- Hooked it right into all their contact forms
- Admin panel so they can actually manage tickets properly
- Automated everything so responses happen faster
- Real-time updates so customers know what's happening

### New Revenue Stream
Created this paid promotions thing that opened up a whole new way to make money:
- Users can request promotions from each other
- Stripe integration for payments (obviously secure)
- Dashboard to manage all the requests
- Analytics to see what's working

## The Tech Stuff

Built it with Next.js, React, Material UI, and TypeScript. Used Jest for testing because you gotta make sure stuff actually works. Stripe handles all the payment processing, and everything's built with reusable components.

## Results

Pretty solid outcomes:
- 50% bump in sales in just the first quarter
- Support is way more streamlined now
- Whole new way to make money with the promotions
- Users are actually happy with the experience now

## What I Learned

This whole thing really showed me how much good design matters for business results. When you actually solve real problems that users have instead of just making things look pretty, you can turn a failing platform into something that actually works.
