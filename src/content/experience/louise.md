## What Was Going On

Louise's CEO Atlal hired me through Upwork for a really urgent draft of their dashboard, but soon it turned into a role where I built their **design system**, their **website**, their **dashboard**, their **features**, you name it I did it, all while learning about IVF and women's health.

## How Things Evolved

What started as an urgent dashboard draft quickly expanded into building their entire frontend ecosystem. I had to dive deep into the healthcare domain, specifically IVF and women's reproductive health, to understand what doctors and patients actually needed.

## What I Built

### Complete Frontend Ecosystem

I built their **design system** in **Tailwind and Storybook** that reflected the brand's identity, including components such as **MultiSelect and Autocomplete**. The design system became the foundation for everything else - the dashboard, the marketing website, and all future features.

### Admin Dashboard for Healthcare

I developed an **admin dashboard** in **React and Next.js** that allowed OB/GYN doctors to manage their patients, including insights, billing, and treatments. The dashboard had to handle sensitive medical data while being intuitive enough for busy healthcare professionals to use efficiently.

### Internationalization Implementation

I added **multilingual capabilities** to the app (i18n), making French speakers feel right at home, which made it easier for doctors from both France and the US to onboard. This involved translating complex medical terminology and adapting workflows for different healthcare systems.

## Technical Implementation

Built with **React**, **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**. **Storybook** for component documentation and development, with i18n support for French and English localization. I established a component-based architecture with accessibility compliance built in from the start. The entire system was designed to handle sensitive healthcare data while maintaining fast performance and intuitive user experience.
